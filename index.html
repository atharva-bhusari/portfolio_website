About Me

I'm a Data Analyst & Engineer with ~3.5 years of experience building and supporting data and ERP systems across healthcare, pharmaceutical, and supply-chain work. Most of my day-to-day lives at the seam between business requirements and working systems — turning what stakeholders actually need into ETL pipelines, ERP reports, and integrations that hold up in production.

A few things I’ve worked on:

  • At RS InfoCon, I build and enhance JD Edwards applications and orchestrations across Order-to-Cash, freight, and EDI workflows — including data-handoff automation that cut downstream turnaround by roughly 30%
  • At Cognizant (client: Merck), I built and monitored Informatica/Oracle ETL pipelines moving ~120K–150K payroll records a day, and worked with QA and business teams to keep reporting reliable
  • At Curanostics, I built HIPAA-compliant pipelines that turned unstructured EPIC EHR data into structured formats for downstream analytics, with FastAPI services on top

Outside of work I build to learn. My most recent project is a Power BI dashboard on the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset — I modeled the raw order data into a star schema using Python and DuckDB, then built DAX measures for delivery and freight performance to surface where orders slow down and what it costs. The full pipeline and model are reproducible from the repo. Before that I built an ERP AI Assistant, a RAG system that answers natural-language questions over JD Edwards documentation. I’ve also co-authored two published papers, including IntelliGenes in Bioinformatics.

I hold an M.S. in Data Science from Rutgers and I’m based in the Milwaukee area. I’m most interested in roles across data engineering, data and business analytics, and AI engineering. Always happy to talk shop with people building similar things.
